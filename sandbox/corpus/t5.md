# Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (T5) (2019)

## Problem
Transfer learning in NLP had fragmented into many task-specific architectures,
objectives, and conventions, making it hard to compare methods or share one model
across tasks.

## Method
T5 casts every NLP task — translation, classification, summarization, question
answering — as text-to-text: the model reads a text input with a task prefix and
produces a text output. With this single format, one encoder-decoder Transformer,
objective, and decoding procedure handles all tasks. The paper runs a large, systematic
study of pretraining objectives, model sizes, datasets, and fine-tuning strategies, and
introduces the large cleaned C4 web corpus for pretraining.

## Key contributions
- A unified text-to-text framing that lets one model and loss serve every task.
- A careful empirical survey of transfer-learning design choices at scale.
- The C4 dataset and the T5 model family as reusable resources.

## Results
T5 achieved state-of-the-art results across many language benchmarks and its
text-to-text formulation became a widely adopted template, influencing instruction
tuning and how later models frame diverse tasks uniformly.
