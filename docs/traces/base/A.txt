FINAL: Claude Shannon was born on April 30, 1916, and passed away on February 24, 2001.

He is widely recognized as the "father of information theory," and three of his key contributions to the field include:

*   **The Mathematical Theory of Communication:** In his seminal 1948 paper, he established the fundamental concepts of information theory, including the quantification of information using the "bit" (binary digit).
*   **Source Coding Theorem:** He proved that for any given source of information, there is a theoretical limit (entropy) to how much the data can be compressed without losing information.
*   **Channel Capacity (Shannon-Hartley Theorem):** He defined the maximum rate at which information can be reliably transmitted over a communication channel in the presence of noise, establishing the fundamental limits of communication systems.
