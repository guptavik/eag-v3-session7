FINAL: The Transformer architecture, introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need," made three key contributions to sequence modeling:

1.  **Elimination of Recurrence:** By replacing recurrent and convolutional layers entirely with self-attention mechanisms, the architecture removes the sequential processing bottleneck inherent in previous models.
2.  **Parallelization:** Because the model computes scaled dot-product attention across the entire sequence simultaneously rather than token-by-token, it enables significantly faster training times through massive parallelization.
3.  **Improved Long-Range Dependency Modeling:** The self-attention mechanism allows each position in a sequence to attend to every other position directly. This structure makes it much easier for the model to learn and capture dependencies between distant positions, overcoming the limitations of traditional models that struggled with long-range context.
