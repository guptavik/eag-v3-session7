FINAL: The Transformer model, as introduced in the "Attention Is All You Need" paper, is primarily recognized for three key contributions that revolutionized sequence transduction:

1.  **Elimination of Recurrence and Convolution:** The Transformer is the first sequence transduction model to rely entirely on self-attention mechanisms to compute representations of its input and output. By eschewing recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs), it avoids the sequential processing bottlenecks inherent in those architectures.
2.  **Full Parallelization:** Because the model does not rely on recurrence, it can process entire sequences in parallel rather than element-by-element. This allows for significantly faster training times compared to previous state-of-the-art models, enabling the training of much larger models on modern hardware.
3.  **Global Dependency Modeling:** Through the use of multi-head self-attention, the model can draw global dependencies between input and output sequences regardless of their distance. This allows the model to capture contextual relationships more effectively than previous architectures, which often struggled with long-range dependencies.
