FINAL: Based on the "Attention Is All You Need" paper, the three key contributions of the Transformer model are:

1.  **Replacement of Recurrence with Self-Attention:** The Transformer eliminates recurrent and convolutional layers entirely, relying instead on self-attention mechanisms to process sequences.
2.  **Parallelization of Computation:** By using scaled dot-product attention, the model computes representations for all positions in a sequence simultaneously, allowing for significantly higher parallelism during training compared to sequential models.
3.  **Improved Long-Range Dependency Modeling:** Because each position attends to every other position directly, the model effectively removes the distance-based limitations of recurrent models, making it much easier to learn dependencies between distant tokens in a sequence.
